# Emergency policy — Harbrook Wharf

## Who to call
In a fire, call 999 first. For all other building emergencies, call the 24-hour on-call line on 020 7946 0456.

## Heating and hot water
Harbrook Wharf has a communal heating system serving every flat. A loss of heating or hot water affecting several flats is treated as urgent, and the heating contractor aims to attend within 4 hours. Residents who are elderly, unwell or have a baby under 12 months can ask the on-call team for a temporary electric heater while repairs are made.

## Keeping residents updated
During any outage affecting more than one flat, the property manager will post updates in the resident app at least every 3 hours until it is resolved.
