# House rules — Harbrook Wharf

## Front doors
Flat front doors are fire doors. They must not be painted a different colour, replaced or altered in any way, and door closers must stay fitted. Door numbers and letterboxes are maintained by the managing agent.

## Noise
Quiet hours are 10:30pm to 7:30am. Residents are asked to use rugs on wooden floors to reduce noise to flats below.

## Bicycles
Bicycles must be kept in the cycle store on the ground floor, not in corridors or on balconies.

## Pets
Pets are allowed with the managing agent's written permission. Dogs must be kept on a lead in communal areas.
