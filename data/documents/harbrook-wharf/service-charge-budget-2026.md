# Service charge budget 2026 — Harbrook Wharf

## Summary of changes
The total service charge budget for 2026 is £96,400, up 7.8% on 2025. The increase is driven mainly by buildings insurance and the new communal heating maintenance contract.

## Buildings insurance
Buildings insurance rises from £18,200 to £21,500 after the policy renewal in January 2026, following higher reinstatement cost valuations across the market.

## Heating maintenance
A new communal heating maintenance contract starts in 2026 at £6,800 a year, replacing call-out repairs, which cost £5,100 in 2025.

## Apportionment
Each flat's share of the service charge is set in its lease and has not changed. Residents can see their own share and payment schedule in the resident app.
