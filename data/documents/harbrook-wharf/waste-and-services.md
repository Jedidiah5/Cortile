# Waste and building services — Harbrook Wharf

## Bin collection
General waste is collected by the council every Wednesday morning and recycling every other Wednesday. If a collection is missed, the managing agent reports it to the council, which normally returns within 2 working days.

## Bin store
The bin store is at the rear of the building. Bags must be placed inside the bins, not on the floor. Overflowing bins are cleared by the cleaning contractor on their next visit, Monday and Thursday.

## Cleaning
Communal areas are cleaned every Monday and Thursday by Brightway Cleaning Ltd.
