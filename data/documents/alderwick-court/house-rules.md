# House rules — Alderwick Court

## Balconies and barbecues
Barbecues, fire pits and any open flames are not permitted on balconies or in communal areas. Two booking-only grill points are available in the courtyard and can be reserved through the resident app. Balconies must not be used for storage, and nothing may be hung over the balcony rail.

## Noise and quiet hours
Quiet hours are 11pm to 7am every day. Please keep music and televisions at a level that cannot be heard in neighbouring flats. Persistent noise complaints should be reported to the property manager, who will contact the flat concerned in writing.

## DIY and building works
Drilling and other noisy DIY are allowed from 9am to 6pm Monday to Friday, and from 10am to 4pm on Saturdays. No noisy works are allowed on Sundays or bank holidays. Any structural work, including removing walls or changing flooring, needs written consent from the managing agent first.

## Parcels
Parcels are received by the concierge desk and logged in the resident app. Residents are notified when a parcel arrives. Parcels are kept for 14 days; after a reminder on day 7, uncollected parcels are returned to the sender on day 14.

## Key fobs and access
Each flat is issued two key fobs. Additional fobs for cleaners, carers or family can be requested through the resident app and cost £25 each. Fobs for non-residents can be set to work only during chosen hours. Lost fobs must be reported straight away so they can be deactivated.

## Bins and recycling
The bin store is on the ground floor next to the car park entrance. General waste and recycling are collected every Tuesday and Friday morning. Bulky items must not be left in the bin store; book a bulky waste collection through the property manager.

## Front doors and appearance
Flat front doors are fire doors and must not be painted, replaced or altered. Door closers must not be removed or disabled.
