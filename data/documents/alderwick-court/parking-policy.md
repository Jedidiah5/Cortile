# Parking policy — Alderwick Court

## Allocated spaces
Each allocated space is linked to a specific flat and shown in the resident app. Spaces may only be used by the residents of that flat or their named visitors.

## Visitor parking
There are four visitor bays near the car park entrance. Visitors must be registered in the resident app and can stay for up to 24 hours.

## Storage and vehicles
Nothing may be stored in parking spaces. Vehicles must be taxed, insured and roadworthy. Abandoned vehicles will be removed after written notice.

## Car park gate
The car park gate opens with the flat's key fob. If the gate is faulty, report it to the property manager in the resident app.
