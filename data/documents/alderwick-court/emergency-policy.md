# Emergency policy — Alderwick Court

## What counts as an emergency
An emergency is anything that puts people at risk or is causing serious damage right now: water coming through a ceiling, a burst pipe, a fire, a smell of gas, someone trapped in a lift, or a complete loss of power to the building.

## Who to call
In a fire, call 999 first. For all other building emergencies, call the 24-hour on-call line on 020 7946 0123. The on-call contractor aims to attend leaks and floods within 2 hours, and lift entrapments within 1 hour. For a gas smell, call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 and leave the building.

## Leaks and floods
If water is coming into your flat, turn off your stopcock if it is safe to do so (it is under the kitchen sink in all flats) and switch off electrics in the affected rooms. The on-call team will trace the source, which may mean entering the flat above. The building insurance covers damage to the structure; residents' contents insurance covers their belongings.

## Keeping residents updated
During any emergency affecting more than one flat, the property manager will post updates in the resident app at least every 2 hours until it is resolved.
