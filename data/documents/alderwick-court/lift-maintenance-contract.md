# Lift maintenance contract summary — Alderwick Court

## Contractor
The passenger lift is maintained by Northfield Lifts Ltd under a comprehensive contract renewed in April 2026. The contract covers parts and labour for all breakdowns.

## Response times
For a lift that is out of service, the contractor attends within 4 hours between 8am and 6pm on weekdays, and within 6 hours at all other times. For a person trapped in the lift, the contractor attends within 1 hour, 24 hours a day.

## Accessibility while the lift is out
While the lift is out of service, step-free access to the car park level is via the ramp at the rear courtyard entrance. Residents who need help with stairs or deliveries can ask the concierge team, who will assist where they can.

## Planned servicing
The lift is serviced on the first Monday of every month between 10am and 12pm, when it may be out of use for up to 2 hours. Residents are notified in the app the week before.
